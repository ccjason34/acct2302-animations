⚠️ Common Trap

NPV discounts future cash flows before deciding. Payback is only a quick screen because it ignores time value and later cash flows.

Real-World Case: Amazon Bets on Cash Flow, Not Earnings

In its 1997 shareholder letter, Amazon told investors it manages for long-term free cash flow, not quarterly accounting earnings — stating that a share is "worth only the present value of [its] future cash flows, not… future earnings." That is exactly the idea behind NPV.

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

NPV translates future cash flows into today's dollars before making an investment decision.

Key Terms
  • Present value: What a future cash flow is worth today.
  • Discount rate: Required return used to value future cash flows.
  • NPV: PV of inflows minus the initial investment.
  • Payback: Time needed to recover the investment.
Takeaway

NPV is in dollars, so it directly estimates value added.

Show The Math

NPV = present value of cash inflows - initial investment. Positive NPV means the project is expected to add value.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain why a future dollar is worth less than a dollar today.
  • Calculate net present value (NPV) and decide accept/reject.
  • Contrast NPV with the payback period.

⚠️ Common Trap

Total Manufacturing Costs ≠ Cost of Goods Manufactured. Total manufacturing costs are everything added this period. COGM is only what was finished, so beginning and ending Work in Process matter.

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Different business types carry different inventory accounts, which changes what students see on the balance sheet.

Key Terms
  • Manufacturer: Makes goods and may have raw materials, WIP, and finished goods.
  • Merchandiser: Buys finished goods for resale.
  • Service business: Sells services and usually has no product inventory.
  • COGM: Cost of completed production transferred out of WIP.
Takeaway

A manufacturer has the longest cost path because it makes the inventory before selling it.

Show The Math

COGM = Beginning WIP + direct materials used + direct labor + manufacturing overhead - Ending WIP.

Learning Objectives

  • Compare the inventory accounts of service, merchandising, and manufacturing firms.
  • Build a Schedule of Cost of Goods Manufactured.
  • Distinguish Total Manufacturing Costs from Cost of Goods Manufactured.

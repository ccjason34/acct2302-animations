Residual Income: Above the Required Return

Residual income asks a dollar question: after the division covers the return the company requires on its assets, how much operating income is left?

Operating income$160,000
Average operating assets$800,000
Required return12%
Project choices+$15,000 or +$10,000 on +$100,000
The project earns 15% ($15,000 / $100,000). That is below the division's current 20% ROI, but above the company's 12% required return.
Step 1

Charge the division for using assets.

$800,000 x 12% = $96,000

This is the required return on the assets the division controls.

Step 2

Subtract that required return from income.

$160,000 - $96,000 = $64,000

The division earned $64,000 above the minimum return expected.

Step 3

Accept projects that add residual income.

$175,000 - $108,000 = $67,000

With the project, residual income rises by $3,000 even though ROI falls.

Common Trap

A lower average ROI does not automatically mean a bad project. If the project earns more than the required return, it adds residual income and creates value.

Real-World Case: Xbox Inside Microsoft

A Yahoo Finance/GameSpot article describes an analyst's argument that Xbox might be better as a separate company. The accounting issue sounds familiar: Xbox may be valuable, but gaming can have lower margins than Microsoft businesses such as Azure or Windows. If Xbox earns a lower ROI than those higher-return businesses, keeping Xbox inside Microsoft can pull down Microsoft's overall average ROI.

Why other units can have high ROI

Software and cloud products can scale. After the platform is built, extra licenses, subscriptions, or cloud usage may add income without the same amount of new assets.

Why Xbox ROI may look lower

Gaming can require heavy investment in consoles, studios, game content, acquisitions, subscriptions, and marketing. More assets and lower margins can reduce ROI.

Company-wide effect

If Azure and Windows have very high ROI, adding a lower-ROI Xbox division can lower Microsoft's overall ROI, even if Xbox is still profitable.

Residual income question: Should Microsoft judge Xbox only by whether it lowers the average ROI, or also ask whether Xbox earns dollars above the required return and supports Microsoft's strategy?

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Residual income helps evaluate investment centers without rejecting useful projects just because they lower average ROI.

Key Terms
  • Required return: the minimum dollar return expected from assets.
  • Residual income: operating income left after the required return.
  • ROI trap: rejecting a good project to protect a high average ROI.
Takeaway

If a project earns more than the required return, residual income increases.

Show The Math

Required return = required rate x average operating assets. Residual income = operating income - required return.

Learning Objectives

  • Calculate the required return on average operating assets.
  • Calculate residual income.
  • Explain why residual income can avoid the ROI trap.

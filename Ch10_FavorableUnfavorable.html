Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Favorable and unfavorable labels need interpretation, not automatic praise or blame.

Key Terms
  • Favorable: A variance that increases profit relative to standard.
  • Unfavorable: A variance that decreases profit relative to standard.
  • Cause: The operating reason behind a variance.
  • Investigation: Looking into variances large enough to matter.
Takeaway

Favorable is not always good if it came from lower quality, delays, or underinvestment.

Show The Math

Variance sign depends on whether actual cost is above or below standard cost. Then interpret the business reason.

Learning Objectives

  • Classify direct materials and direct labor variances as favorable or unfavorable.
  • Investigate the cause behind a variance, not just its label.
  • Recognize that variances are interconnected — one can cause another.

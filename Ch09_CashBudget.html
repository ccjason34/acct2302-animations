Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

A company can be profitable on paper and still run short of cash.

Key Terms
  • Cash receipts: Cash expected to come in.
  • Cash disbursements: Cash expected to be paid out.
  • Minimum cash balance: Lowest cash level management wants to keep.
  • Financing: Borrowing or repayment needed to manage cash.
Takeaway

The cash budget is about timing: when cash comes in and when cash leaves.

Show The Math

Cash before financing = beginning cash + receipts - payments. Borrowing needed = minimum cash balance - cash before financing.

Learning Objectives

  • Calculate cash collections and cash payments by month.
  • Prepare a cash budget with beginning cash, cash receipts, cash disbursements, and ending cash.
  • Identify when borrowing is needed to meet a minimum cash balance.

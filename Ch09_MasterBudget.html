⚠️ Common Trap

If the sales forecast is wrong, the linked budgets are wrong too. Production also adjusts for desired inventory, so production does not always equal sales.

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

The master budget links operating plans together so one assumption flows through the company.

Key Terms
  • Sales budget: Starting point for many operating budgets.
  • Production budget: Units to produce based on sales and desired inventory.
  • Direct materials budget: Materials needed for production.
  • Cash budget: Expected receipts, payments, and financing.
Takeaway

One sales forecast can affect production, materials, labor, overhead, and cash.

Show The Math

Required production = budgeted sales + desired ending inventory - beginning inventory.

Learning Objectives

  • Order the budgets in the master budget, starting with sales.
  • Compute required production = sales + desired ending inventory − beginning inventory.
  • Trace how one budget's output feeds the next.

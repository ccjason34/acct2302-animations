⚠️ Common Trap

Revenue covers variable costs first. The remaining contribution margin covers fixed costs, then profit. Total costs already include fixed costs. Changing sales volume changes profit; it does not move the break-even point unless price or costs also change.

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Break-even tells the sales level where the business stops losing money.

Key Terms
  • Contribution margin: Sales minus variable costs.
  • Break-even point: Sales where profit equals zero.
  • Fixed cost: Cost that must be covered before profit starts.
  • CVP: Cost-volume-profit analysis.
Takeaway

Every unit sold contributes toward fixed costs first; after break-even, it contributes to profit.

Show The Math

Break-even units = fixed costs / contribution margin per unit. Break-even sales dollars = fixed costs / contribution margin ratio.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the break-even point on a cost-volume-profit graph.
  • Calculate break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin per unit.
  • Explain how price, variable costs, and fixed costs change the graph and break-even point.

⚠️ Common Trap

Beware the "ROI trap": a manager with a 20% ROI may reject a solid 15% project just to protect their average, even though it beats the 12% required return. Residual income avoids that trap by measuring dollars above the required return.

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

ROI can improve because margins rise, assets are used better, or both.

Key Terms
  • ROI: Operating income divided by average operating assets.
  • Margin: Operating income divided by sales.
  • Turnover: Sales divided by average operating assets.
  • Residual income: Income above the required return on assets.
Takeaway

ROI is not one lever. It combines profitability and asset use.

Show The Math

ROI = margin x turnover. Residual income = operating income - required return on operating assets.

Learning Objectives

  • Compute ROI as profit margin × asset turnover.
  • Explain two ways to improve ROI.
  • Calculate residual income and see why it can beat ROI for decisions.

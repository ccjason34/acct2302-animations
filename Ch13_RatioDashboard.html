Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Ratios turn financial statements into signals about profitability, liquidity, and performance.

Key Terms
  • Liquidity: Ability to pay short-term obligations.
  • Profitability: Ability to earn income.
  • Turnover: How efficiently assets are used.
  • Balanced scorecard: Financial and nonfinancial performance measures together.
Takeaway

A ratio is a signal, not a final answer. Ask what changed and why.

Show The Math

Common ratio logic: numerator / denominator. Always compare with history, industry, or a target.

Learning Objectives

  • Compute liquidity, profitability, and solvency ratios.
  • Interpret what each ratio says about the company.
  • Explain why nonfinancial measures (the balanced scorecard) matter too.

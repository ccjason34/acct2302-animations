⚠️ Common Trap

Product costs flow through inventory first. Period costs skip inventory and are expensed right away. A product cost becomes Cost of Goods Sold only when the item is sold.

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

This is the basic map for where manufacturing costs go before they become expense on the income statement.

Key Terms
  • Raw materials: Materials waiting to be used in production.
  • WIP: Partly finished units.
  • Finished goods: Completed units not sold yet.
  • COGS: Product cost expensed when goods are sold.
Takeaway

Do not expense product costs immediately. They become expense when the product is sold.

Show The Math

Beginning inventory + additions - ending inventory = cost transferred out. Costs move from raw materials to WIP to finished goods to COGS.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the three inventory accounts costs flow through: Raw Materials → Work in Process → Finished Goods.
  • Trace direct materials, direct labor, and manufacturing overhead into Work in Process.
  • Explain why a product cost becomes Cost of Goods Sold only when the product is sold.

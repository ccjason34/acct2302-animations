Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Overhead is often assigned before actual overhead is known, so managers use an estimated rate.

Key Terms
  • Predetermined overhead rate: Estimated overhead divided by estimated activity.
  • Applied overhead: Rate times actual activity.
  • Overapplied: Applied overhead exceeds actual overhead.
  • Underapplied: Applied overhead is less than actual overhead.
Takeaway

Estimate the rate first, apply overhead during the period, then true up the difference later.

Show The Math

POHR = estimated overhead / estimated activity. Applied OH = POHR x actual activity. Difference = actual OH - applied OH.

Learning Objectives

  • Calculate a predetermined overhead rate from estimated cost and estimated activity.
  • Apply overhead during the year using actual activity.
  • Determine over- or under-applied overhead and adjust Cost of Goods Sold.

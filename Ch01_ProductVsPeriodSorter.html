Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Managers need to know which costs attach to products and which costs belong to the period.

Key Terms
  • Product cost: Manufacturing cost: direct materials, direct labor, or manufacturing overhead.
  • Period cost: Selling or administrative cost expensed in the current period.
  • Prime cost: Direct materials plus direct labor.
  • Conversion cost: Direct labor plus manufacturing overhead.
Takeaway

Ask whether the cost helps make the product. If yes, it is usually a product cost.

Show The Math

Prime cost = DM + DL. Conversion cost = DL + MOH. Direct labor appears in both formulas because it helps make the product and convert materials.

Learning Objectives

  • Classify a cost as product (DM, DL, MOH) or period (selling & administrative).
  • Explain why product costs hit the balance sheet first and period costs hit the income statement now.
  • Compute prime cost (DM + DL) and conversion cost (DL + MOH).

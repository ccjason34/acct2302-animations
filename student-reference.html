ACCT 2302 Student Reference

Terms And Formulas You Will Keep Reusing

A quick reference for students who are new to accounting. Use this beside the animations when a term or formula slows you down.

Plain-Language Terms

WIPWork in Process. Products started but not finished yet.
COGMCost of Goods Manufactured. Finished production moved out of WIP.
Applied overheadOverhead assigned to jobs using a predetermined rate.
Contribution marginSales minus variable costs. The amount left to cover fixed costs and profit.
Relevant costA future cost that changes between decision alternatives.
Opportunity costBenefit given up when choosing one option over another.
Relevant rangeThe activity level where the cost formula is expected to behave normally.
Traceable fixed costA fixed cost caused by one segment and avoidable if that segment disappears.
Common fixed costA shared fixed cost that usually remains even if one segment is dropped.
Denominator activityThe planned activity level used to compute a fixed overhead rate.
NPVNet present value. Present value of future cash flows minus the investment.
IRRThe discount rate that makes NPV equal zero.

Core Formulas

Costs And Product Costing
Prime costDirect materials + Direct labor
Conversion costDirect labor + Manufacturing overhead
COGMBeginning WIP + Total manufacturing costs - Ending WIP
Predetermined OH rateEstimated overhead / Estimated activity
Applied overheadPredetermined OH rate x Actual activity
ABC activity rateActivity cost pool / Activity driver total
Cost Behavior And CVP
Cost formulaTotal cost = Fixed cost + (Variable cost per unit x Units)
High-low variable cost(High cost - Low cost) / (High activity - Low activity)
Contribution margin per unitSelling price per unit - Variable cost per unit
Break-even unitsFixed costs / Contribution margin per unit
Target-profit units(Fixed costs + Target profit) / CM per unit
Margin of safetyActual sales - Break-even sales
Decisions, Budgets, And Performance
Special order profitSpecial price - Incremental cost - Opportunity cost
NPVPresent value of inflows - Initial investment
Cash before financingBeginning cash + Receipts - Payments
Borrowing neededMinimum cash balance - Cash before financing
Price variance(Actual price - Standard price) x Actual quantity
Quantity variance(Actual quantity - Standard quantity) x Standard price
ROIOperating income / Average operating assets
ROI decompositionMargin x Turnover
Required return on assetsRequired rate x Average operating assets
Residual incomeOperating income - Required return on assets
Segment marginContribution margin - Traceable fixed costs
Operating cash flow - directOperating cash receipts - Operating cash payments
Operating cash flow - indirectNet income + Noncash expenses +/- working capital changes

When A Problem Feels Stuck

First ask: is this a cost-flow problem, a behavior problem, or a decision problem? Cost-flow problems track where costs go. Behavior problems separate fixed and variable parts. Decision problems keep only future amounts that change.

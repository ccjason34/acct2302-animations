⚠️ Common Trap

Favorable means actual cost was lower than standard (good for cost), unfavorable means higher. The middle column is the key: it keeps actual pounds but switches to standard price, so the first gap is only price and the second gap is only quantity.

Before Practice

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Variance columns separate price/rate problems from quantity/efficiency problems.

Key Terms
  • Standard cost: Expected cost for expected input.
  • Actual cost: What actually happened.
  • Price variance: Difference caused by paying a different price.
  • Quantity variance: Difference caused by using a different amount.
Takeaway

Use actual quantity for price variance and standard price for quantity variance.

Show The Math

Price variance = (AP - SP) x AQ. Quantity variance = (AQ - SQ) x SP.

Learning Objectives

  • Build the three columns: AQ×AP, AQ×SP, and SQ×SP, assuming pounds purchased were used.
  • Compute the price variance and the quantity variance.
  • Label each variance favorable or unfavorable.

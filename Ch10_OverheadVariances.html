Overhead Variances: Four Signals

Direct materials and labor variances are only part of standard costing. Overhead adds variable and fixed overhead signals.

Assumption: this page uses direct labor hours as the overhead activity base.

Variable overhead asks two questions

SpendingDid the overhead rate per hour change?
EfficiencyDid we use more hours than allowed?

Fixed overhead asks two questions

BudgetDid fixed OH spending exceed the budget?
VolumeDid production volume differ from the planned base?

Check The Idea In Plain Language

Terms & formulas
Why This Matters

Overhead variances split one big overhead difference into signals managers can investigate.

Key Terms
  • Variable overhead (VOH) spending: Variable overhead rate difference.
  • Variable overhead (VOH) efficiency: Activity hours used compared with hours allowed.
  • Fixed overhead (FOH) budget: Actual fixed overhead compared with budget.
  • Fixed overhead (FOH) volume: Production volume compared with denominator activity.
Takeaway

Variable overhead looks at rate and hours; fixed overhead separates spending from production volume.

Show The Math

VOH spending = (AR - SR) x AH. VOH efficiency = (AH - SH) x SR. FOH budget = actual FOH - budgeted FOH.

Learning Objectives

  • Separate variable overhead spending and efficiency variances.
  • Separate fixed overhead budget and volume variances.
  • Interpret what each overhead variance is trying to signal.
